# Applied AI for Health Systems — DESIGN.md

12 VENTURES is quiet chrome (nav / footer). Product name is the hero brand signal.

## One job

Get a health system exec or ops leader to **Book a conversation**.

## Persuasion arc

1. Hero — Applied AI for Health Systems; measurable ops impact; Book CTA
2. Method — opportunity → production → measurable impact
3. Patient Access proof — voice agent KPIs (coded diagrams)
4. Workforce Training proof — training economics / competency (coded diagrams)
5. Where we go next — shared roadmap (EHR scheduling/registration + scale)
6. Close — Book a conversation
7. Footer — 12 VENTURES · hello@12ventures.io

## Visual signature — daylight campus

- Ground: warm paper `#F4F1EA` / `#EBE6DC` (campus daylight — intentional; not cream+terracotta AI cliché)
- Ink: charcoal `#1A1D21` / soft `#4A5058`
- Accent: restrained blue-green `#2F6F6A` (trust, live ops) — sparingly
- Good/metric lift: `#3D8B74`
- Surface cards only when they frame interactive or dense diagram chrome
- Display: `Fraunces` (or Georgia). Body: system UI. Labels: `ui-monospace`
- Photography: daylight campus / clinical ops atmosphere; muted; no text in pixels; no logos

## Diagram language (coded)

Prefer built-in React/SVG boards over fake dashboards:

- Abandonment: 9% → 2% meter or arc
- Coverage: 100% / 24/7 pulse
- Capacity: FTE impact vs concurrent capacity
- Training: time/cost −50%, 2× readiness, +40% competency
- Arc strip: opportunity → production → impact
- Next: short roadmap steps (shared section)

Animate reveals like AI2P: left copy first, then diagram; stagger checks/meters; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Anti-slop bans

- Inter/Roboto as display voice
- Purple-on-white / indigo glow / glassmorphism soup
- Pill-stat hero strips as the whole hero composition
- Three identical icon feature cards
- Stock smiling clinicians, neon AI brains
- Invented hospital names or numbers beyond the PDF
- Naming Royce or any personal AE on-page

## Image-as-surface

Hero is full-bleed daylight still. Type on a light scrim or left paper panel over photo. Proof stills can be split or surface — prefer surface for case openers.

## Copy rules

- Literal above the fold: Applied AI for Health Systems is [category] for [audience].
- Prefer periods/commas over em dashes.
- Anonymize: “Southern California health system”, “Los Angeles community health system”.
- CTA label: Book a conversation. Calendly URL constant (placeholder until real).
- Email only in footer: hello@12ventures.io
