How to read a score

A score describes how ready that topic would look if surveyors arrived tomorrow. It is not a grade of the paperwork in the folder. The folder lists the rules. The score is filled in only after someone checks the hospital against those rules.

1Not in placeA surveyor could not be shown this.
2Major gapsSomething is written down. Daily proof is missing.
3Partly in placeSome of it would pass. Some would be cited.
4Mostly in placeMost of it can be shown. A few holes remain.
5Survey readyThe requirement is met and easy to pull up.
