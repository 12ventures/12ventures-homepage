Where they agree

  • A model proposes and a person confirms. Confidence never counts as acceptance.
  • Every fact keeps its exact source text and location.
  • An EP is its own thing, part of its standard.
  • The edition is stated as a fact and never guessed from a file name.
  • Nothing becomes satisfies automatically, and a gap stays queryable instead of hidden.

Where they differ

  • The POC normalizes below the EP into obligations. We stop at the EP's verbatim text.
  • The POC fits any source with one recursive provision. We add a type per source.
  • The POC separates a document from its content, and a designed control from an adopted one. Our policy is the document.
  • We keep the workbook's printed columns and CFR mappings. The POC drops them.
  • Ours is enforced and populated at scale. The POC's is a proposal its own data does not yet satisfy.
