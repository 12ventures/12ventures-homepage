What we need from you

  1. Would you ask this? Strike anything you would never actually ask. A short list of real questions beats a long list of plausible ones.
  2. Who asks it? Name the role — compliance, accreditation, nursing education, HR, a unit manager. Different askers want different answers to the same words.
  3. What did we miss? The questions you ask most, that aren't here. These matter more than anything on the list.

Each question is tagged with the relationships a knowledge graph would need in order to answer it, and with whether the documents we currently hold can support it. That second tag is the honest part: most of these we cannot answer yet, and the pattern of what's missing tells us what to build and what to ask you for next.

What we do with your answers

The questions you keep become the specification for the ontology — the set of relationships the graph is actually built to support. The ones you strike, we don't build for. The ones you add, we come back to you about.

We would rather build for eight questions someone genuinely asks than twenty-five we invented.

Every statement here about the corpus comes from the two document sets we hold and their manifests, verified file by file. Nothing about your policies, approvals or review cycles was inferred or filled in — where a fact was absent, the question is marked blocked rather than answered. The questions themselves are our proposals, and none of them has been validated with anyone at MLKCH yet. That is what this document is for.
